# ACC distance bars — independent owner-video validation, October 14

The owner's `acc_distance.MOV` directly confirms **all four distance-bar
settings with and without a lead-vehicle icon**. The existing September 29
decoder is correct: no runtime mapping or dashboard change is needed.
This closes the visual-validation question for the engaged distance settings;
it does not assign meanings to unused graphic indices or measure lead range.

## Mapping and direct visual evidence

On C-CAN, standard ID `0x5A0`, DLC 8, let `index = byte6 >> 2` (zero-based bytes).
While the state is engaged (`((byte6 & 1) << 2) | (byte7 >> 6) == 2`):

| Visible bars | Lead-vehicle icon shown: index / byte6 | No lead-vehicle icon: index / byte6 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 / `18` | 10 / `28` |
| 2 | 7 / `1C` | 11 / `2C` |
| 3 | 8 / `20` | 12 / `30` |
| 4 | 9 / `24` | 13 / `34` |

The byte values are hexadecimal. This is a state-dependent graphic index,
not an independent two-bit bar counter. Losing the lead icon at the same
four-bar setting changes index 9 to 13, without changing the selected gap.
Keep the state/index lookup in `ccan_powertrain.ACC_HUD_FAMILIES`; do not
replace it with a modulo-four formula across all graphic indices.

The cluster's **ACC Ready wording after cancellation** corresponds to the
API's `standby` state (raw 5, remembered set speed). Initial activation before
SET is the distinct raw-1 `ready` state. At video position 64 the temporary
"Speed control ready" popup covers the distance graphic; that still is not
used as direct visual evidence for four bars, although the raw index is 5.
In standby, the decoder deliberately withholds `acc.lead_vehicle` rather
than interpreting the absent lead icon as active target tracking.

The original [September 28 finding](2026-09-28_acc_owner_reference_drive.md)
remains the authority for other adaptive families and fixed cruise. This
video additionally verifies standby at one/four bars and no-lead override at
four bars. It does not independently exercise every bar in every state.

## Timing and counterexamples

The video metadata starts at `2026-10-14T18:27:30Z`, also recorded as
`2026-10-14T13:27:30-0500`. Use its relative presentation times against the
recorder's UTC timestamps. The cluster itself displays `07/23/22`; that
displayed date is not a usable time reference for this recording.

Multiple independent changes corroborate the metadata alignment: lead-car
distance changes at relative 15.154, 17.034, 18.995 and 21.995 seconds; the
lead icon disappearing at 30.629; ACC off at 46.229; reactivation at 63.710;
and the no-lead four-to-one sequence at 68.589, 72.553 and 75.229 seconds.
The report compares settled displays, not exact video/CAN frame synchrony.

One inspected boundary sample is explicitly excluded from the stable-sample
fixture: the still requested at 106 s has actual PTS 105.986667 s and still
shows **one** bar, whereas CAN announced index 11 (**two** bars) at relative
105.912914 s, approximately 74 ms earlier. File-start precision and cluster
rendering delay have not been independently separated. This is not evidence
for changing the two-bar mapping, which is directly visible at 74 s. Do not
claim zero display latency or label every video frame by the newest CAN frame.

Raw buttons provide additional consistency checks:

- `0x2FA` B0 bit6 decreases the setting; B1 bit7 increases it. Effective presses
  precede the expected single-index changes in both engaged families.
- Decrease presses at relative 77.199 and 79.099 s leave index 10 unchanged:
  the one-bar limit clamps instead of wrapping.
- Three decrease presses while ACC is off (47.419, 48.559, 49.939 s) do not
  alter the setting retained on reactivation: raw ready index 5, then engaged
  index 13 and visually four bars at 68 s. A button press alone is not a
  publishable distance setting.

These are display settings, not a measured distance to the car ahead.
The [2022 ProMaster owner's manual, printed page 87](https://vehicleinfo.mopar.com/assets/publications/en-us/Ram/2022/ProMaster/P135558_22_VF_OM_EN_USC_DIGITAL.pdf)
also describes four selectable distance settings; it supplies no CAN layout.
The raw frames and this owner's video establish the byte mapping on this van.
