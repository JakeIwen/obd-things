# Fuel tank level: B-CAN `0x356` byte 0

## Result

B-CAN `0x356` byte 0 is the fuel tank level in percent (unsigned, 1–100). It is a
candidate. Between about 45 and 80 % it is linear at about 22.9 US gal per 100 % (a metered fill,
below); above 80 % each percent takes more fuel, so it is not a linear gallons gauge near full.

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame | B-CAN (125 kbit/s) `0x356`, DLC 8, 4.0 Hz while the bus is awake |
| Field | byte 0, unsigned, percent (`0x63` = 99) |
| Other bytes | 1–7 near-constant (`0B 80 00/01 00 …`); not part of this metric |
| Zero | raw `0` appears briefly at key-off and wake (payload `000F2001…`). Treated as no value. |
| Valid range | 1–100 published; raw above 100 is rejected |
| Telemetry metric | `fuel.tank_level`, unit `%`, candidate, receive-only (`projects/vehicle_data/fuel_level.py`) |

## Evidence

- The 2026-11-15 drive (capture `tmp/captures/three_bus_drive/broker-drive/broker-drive-20261115T224946048875`,
  archived under `/mnt/EXFAT512/obd-things/`) fell smoothly from 98–99 to 48–49 over 114 minutes:
  53 distinct levels, Spearman correlation −0.99.
- Against fuel burned from the C-CAN `0x2ED` fuel rate the fall is linear: level = 98.9 − 4.82 × gal,
  RMS 1.96 counts.
- The owner then filled 13.56 gal to the brim (tank about 25 gal), which read as about 25.5 gal per
  100 % (superseded by the metered fill below, which separates the slow top band).
- Earlier drives show within-drive declines and jumps at fills: end 11 on Nov 1 against start 95 on
  Nov 12, and end 41 (04:27Z) against start 99 (06:17Z) on Nov 15.
- Post-fill confirmation (2026-11-15, receive-only `candump` on the B-CAN interface): the first
  `0x356` frame at the next wake, 18:46:44 MST, read `64 0B 80 01 10 00 00 00` (100 %), against 48–49
  at the preceding key-off. No raw `0` was seen at that wake, and the value stayed 100 while
  the engine started.
- Key on, engine off (2026-11-15 23:26 MST, receive-only `candump` on the B-CAN interface, 705
  frames over 3 min): the first `0x356` frame was raw `0` (`00 0F 40 01 …`), then 99 % from 2.2 s
  after wake, steady to the end. Both frames broadcast with the ignition alone; the broker as deployed that night published
  them only while the engine ran (its companion receiver followed the B-CAN auxiliary helper).
- Metered fill (owner, reported 2026-11-17; readings from this metric on the dashboard): 45 %
  (it had just ticked down from 46), then 8.000 gal pumped, then 80 % about 30 s later with the key
  on, then 100 % at the first click after 14.353 gal in total; topping off took it to 15.320 gal.
  - 45 → 80 %: 35 points for 8.000 gal, 4.38 points per gal, about **22.9 gal per 100 %**
    (22.5–23.5 allowing for whole-percent rounding at both ends).
  - 80 → 100 %: 20 points for 6.353 gal, 3.15 points per gal (about 32 gal per 100 % at that
    rate): the top fifth of the scale fills about 40 % slower per point.
  - At those rates 0 % to the first click spans about 24.7 gal (80 × 0.229 + 6.35), assuming the
    mid-range rate holds below 45 %.
  - The 80 % reading came 30 s after key-on. If the module smooths the level, the settled value
    could be a point or so higher, which would raise the mid-range rate slightly.
- One unexplained reading: the drive starting 2026-11-14T05:17Z began at 48 between drives that
  ended at 86 and began at 88.

## Open points

- Settled by the metered fill: the earlier 25.5 gal per 100 % came from a fill that crossed the
  slow top band, not from a low `0x2ED` scale. The pump's mid-range 22.9 gal per 100 % is within
  about 4 % of the `0x2ED` integral (10.74 gal over 49 points, about 22 gal per 100 %), so the
  1.131 fuel-rate scale stands. The Nov 15 fill (13.56 gal from 48–49 %) also fits: 31 points at
  0.229 gal plus 6.35 gal for the top band is about 13.4 gal.
- Still open: a direct `0x2ED` check over the 45–80 % band alone (the Nov 15 drive started at 99 %,
  inside the slow band), and whether 0 % is the cluster's empty mark (the owner reports 2–3 gal
  left when the gauge reads empty).
- Raw `0` was seen at the key-off end of a drive and in the first 2 s of the 23:26 key-on wake, but
  not at the post-fill wake; all are handled as no value.
- The Nov 14 05:17Z start of 48 is unexplained.

## Distance to empty: B-CAN `0x760` bytes 4-5

Telemetry metric `fuel.range`, unit miles, candidate, receive-only (`fuel_range.py`).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Frame | B-CAN `0x760`, DLC 6, 1.0 Hz while the bus is awake; the same frame carries the ICS-local odometer (`u17be@8`), which is neither decoded nor published here |
| Field | `raw = (b4 << 3) \| (b5 >> 5)`: unsigned 11 bits, miles. The low 5 bits of b5 were 0 in every capture. |
| No value | raw `0x7FF` (2047), seen at wake and key-off (payload `FFF15C8EFFE0`). Treated like the fuel level's raw 0: the previous reading is kept. |
| Quality | `candidate`: the one direct check is a single owner reading, so it stays at the lowest vocabulary level (`verified` is for repeated or video-referenced decodes) |

Evidence:

- The 2026-11-15 drive (15:49-17:43 MST) went 281 to 126 while the tank level fell 99 to 49 %, a fall of
  155 mi over the 10.74 gal that the `0x2ED` fuel rate integrates to, about 14.4 mi of range per
  gallon burned, in line with the drive's 12-14 mpg.
- After the 13.56 gal fill it read 262 at wake (18:46:47 MST) and 261 with the engine running.
- The owner read 260 mi on the cluster at that time: a single-point confirmation, consistent with
  the 1-2 mi drift between those readings.
- Key on, engine off at 23:26 MST the same night: raw `0x7FF` for the first frame, then 260 for 3 min
  (174 frames).

Open: no second cluster comparison yet, and whether the sender smooths or recomputes the value (it
fell about 14.4 mi per gallon burned, not a fixed distance per percent) is unknown.
